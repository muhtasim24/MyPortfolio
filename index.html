About Me

Hi, I’m Muhtasim, a software engineer in New York with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University at Buffalo. I love building things that solve real problems and help people, and I’m happiest when what I’ve made is something someone uses. That’s what drew me to software: you can start with a problem, build the solution, and see it work.

I’m always learning. I pick up new tools whenever a project calls for them, and I’ve worked on everything from an e-commerce platform that replaced a small business’s manual ordering to interactive experiences for music artists. I like working with people, whether that’s clients, designers or artists, because the best solutions come from understanding what they actually need. Outside of coding, I love going to the gym and training people to reach their fitness potential, and I’m active in NYC Tech Pickleball and a few run clubs. I also enjoy going to events around New York to meet people and learn something new.
